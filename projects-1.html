Project 1 -- This Portfolio

Home

This portfolio was my first CT project. Before creating this, I had no idea what HTML or CSS even meant. Whilst I am still a beginner, I can now comfortably navigate some coding terminology, and also understand important ideas, such as that websites are just files and folder, and that they are made of different boxes (header, main, footer, container, etc). I made this portfolio to demonstrate my skills, and am proud of how it has turned out.

Challenges

Whilst creating this portfolio, I struggled with many challenges. From figuring out how to center a link, to making something not turn purple, I endeavoured until I reached my goal, or at least an attainable semblance of it. One of the most challenging parts of this project was the image in the heading, easily taking over two hours of my time. I had no clue how to embed an image, or change the positioning of a div, and I'm still pretty uncertain. Luckily for me, I am able to access the culmination of the whole world's knpwledge, Google. The amount of different lines of code I wrote, despertely trying to change something, anything with the leafy image, to stubborn to give up because I thought 'it would look cool'. Little did I know, even when I would get it working slightly how I wanted it, I would shrink my screen to check the flexbox, and everything would fall apart. I have fifteen different bundles of CSS. Fifteen. That's over double the amount I used for any other, smaller learning project. If you showed me this a few months ago, I would have no clue what any of it means, and if you told me I would be able to do and understand it all in less than three months, I would have thought you were crazy. But here I am. The creation of this portfolio, though riddled with challenges, amateurish, and still missing some key components, was a valuable test and lesson, honing my skills and improving my understanding, allowing me to be content with the work I have done. For now.
