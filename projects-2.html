Home

This was the second major project that my class underwent, learning how to sort and refine large amounts of data. I created a dashboard on google slides, of some of the most streamed songs ever.

Challenges

Whilst creating this google sheet and dashboard, I struggled with many challenges. From figuring out how to sort data, turn it into a table to sort it easier, and selectively pick what information should be included, to finding different graphs and charts to effectively convey my answers. It is not without flaws, such as having no title on my table chart, or my 'streams compared to released year' chart being difficult to understand. However, I have overcome and learnt how to utilise different tools, even if there is still more to know, such as splicers.
